PramaLex analysis demonstration corpus

This dataset contains 1,000 structured civil/property-law scenario records for demonstrating search, ranking, issue analysis, and evidence-gap workflows.
It is kept separate from the source-verified judgment corpus and should not be cited as a court judgment or legal precedent.
On Windows, place this folder at D:\PramaLexData\demo\ if you prefer to keep runtime data outside the project directory.
